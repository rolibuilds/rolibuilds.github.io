Corporate Services Automation Platform (CSAP)

CSAP is my personal portfolio project, built around a realistic business problem: how to manage requests that require both approval and financial control without relying on disconnected emails and spreadsheets. It uses Dataverse and Power Automate to connect structured request data, financial validation, exchange rates, budget controls and approval workflows in one traceable process.

Current capability

  • Financial validation
  • Multi-currency handling
  • FX-rate maintenance
  • Budget-capacity checks
  • Approval routing
  • Clarification and exception handling
  • Traceable approval history

How it was built

I worked through the requirements, process design, data model and financial rules before building the Dataverse foundation and Power Automate workflows. Testing has been part of the work from the beginning, and I changed both the design and the scope when the evidence showed that I should.

How I used AI

  • Reviewed and trimmed the proposed MVP scope; extra features stayed out until there was a clear business need.
  • Kept testing after the first checks passed and found an implementation error that would otherwise have gone unnoticed.
  • With one real account behind all three test users, I designed a test for actions the flow must block (approving at the wrong approval level, approving my own request) and verified that the guards worked inside the automation, not only in the user interface.
  • Testing exposed a bug where released holds were not cleared for rejected and cancelled requests; I fixed it and re-ran the checks.
