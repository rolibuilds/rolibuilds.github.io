What I Bring

  • Finance & IT Controlling

    Nearly seven years of experience in planning, forecasting, budget control, management reporting and financial decision support, working closely with both finance and IT organisations.

    Proof

    • CAPEX and OPEX planning, forecasting and budget control across a large enterprise IT portfolio
    • Financial analysis and decision support for senior and executive stakeholders
    • Bottom-up planning methodology and plan-versus-actual monitoring

    Explore my OTP career

  • Process Improvement

    My experience comes from practical changes: introducing a more structured forecasting approach, helping shape standardised controlling processes and replacing repetitive reporting work with better tools.

    Proof

    • Introduced a structured forecasting methodology
    • Contributed to controlling standardisation through workshops, stakeholder interviews and requirements gathering
    • Helped move recurring financial reporting from an Excel VBA solution to Power BI

    See the reporting transformation

  • Automation & Digitalisation

    I started by automating individual tasks and reports. I now build solutions that connect data, business rules, decisions and financial controls.

    Proof

    • Automated recurring customer presentation preparation with PowerPoint VBA
    • Built an Excel VBA solution for monthly financial reporting that was later adopted by another controlling team
    • Designed and built a Power Platform solution for controlled financial requests and approvals

    Follow my automation journey

  • Working with AI

    I use Copilot and Claude to explore ideas, challenge assumptions and support the work. I decide what we build, put it together, test it and take responsibility for the result.

    Proof

    • Kept an additional financial feature out of the first release until there was a clear business need for it
    • Continued testing after the initial checks had passed and uncovered an implementation error that would otherwise have gone unnoticed
    • Improved the testing approach to focus on meaningful evidence rather than repetitive checks

    See how I work

  • Building from Ambiguity

    I am comfortable starting without a ready-made solution. I break an unclear problem into decisions, buildable components and tests until something useful begins to take shape.

    Proof

    • Turned a broad financial-process idea into the data model, business rules and workflows behind CSAP
    • Learnt Excel VBA to solve a recurring reporting problem rather than waiting for a ready-made tool
    • Took this portfolio from an initial concept to a designed, tested and published website

    Explore CSAP

  • Capability Building

    I enjoy helping people become more confident in their own expertise and more willing to improve the way they work.

    Proof

    • Led the professional development of a specialist IT controlling team
    • Encouraged practical learning in automation, data, SQL and Microsoft Power Platform
    • Delivered structured billing-process knowledge transfer across teams and implementations

    Explore the career story
